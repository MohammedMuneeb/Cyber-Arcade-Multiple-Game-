Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Mini Arcade Multiple Games (referred to as the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

The App is designed as an offline-first arcade gaming platform. We do not collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, email, phone number, or physical address) on any private servers.

  • Gameplay Scores & Progress: Game high scores, win streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads). Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable device data to serve and personalize ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • Approximate geographical location / IP address
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, view duration)

For more details on how Google uses data collected through AdMob, please visit:
Google Advertising Privacy & Terms

3. Children’s Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Advertising networks integrated into this app are configured in compliance with applicable laws, including the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

4. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to request and display ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

5. Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You can manage or opt-out of personalized ads on your Android device at any time:

  • Go to your device's Settings > Google > Ads
  • Enable "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
